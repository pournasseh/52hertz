# Community conduct

52Hertz is intended to help people speak, especially people who have had less
access to media. Its community should make that participation safer, not add
another barrier.

Be respectful and specific. Critique work without attacking people. Do not
harass, threaten, demean or discriminate against someone, and do not publish
another person's private information or identity without their clear consent.
Take extra care with reports or examples involving activists, vulnerable
communities and identifying media.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, limit participation, or
ban people whose conduct puts others at risk. Serious incidents and anything
that would expose private information should be reported through a private
repository channel rather than a public issue. Maintainers should protect the
reporter's privacy, review the available evidence and respond proportionately.

This applies in the project's repositories, issue trackers, reviews and other
spaces represented as part of the 52Hertz community.
